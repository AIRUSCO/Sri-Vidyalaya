Which combination should students choose in 2nd PUC? If the student is still studying 1st PUC or entering 2nd PUC, subject combinations can influence future opportunities. Common Karnataka Commerce combinations include: Combination Subjects Best suited for CEBA Computer Science, Economics, Business Studies, Accountancy IT, BCA, Business Analytics SEBA Statistics, Economics, Business Studies, Accountancy Banking, Finance, Economics, Analytics MSBA Basic Mathematics, Statistics, Business Studies, Accountancy CA, Finance, Statistics, Analytics HEBA History, Economics, Business Studies, Accountancy Management, Law, General Commerce The exact combinations offered vary by college. Presidency PU Hebbal+2 My suggestion: • Choose CEBA if the student likes computers and technology. • Choose SEBA if the student likes banking, economics and financial analysis. • Choose MSBA if the student is comfortable with mathematics and wants quantitative career options. • Choose HEBA if the student prefers business, management and humanities. Do not select a combination only because it is considered popular. Check eligibility for the intended degree and professional course. 3. What preparation can students do during 2nd PUC? Students should not wait until graduation to start learning professional skills. Here is a practical preparation plan. 2nd PUC career preparation roadmap 1. Stage 1 – Academic foundation o Accountancy fundamentals o Business Studies o Economics o Basic Mathematics and Statistics o Business English 2. Stage 2 – Computer skills o MS Word o MS Excel (formulas, charts, tables) o PowerPoint o Google Sheets o Professional email writing 3. Stage 3 – Career-specific skills Select one learning pathway: o Accounts: Tally Prime, GST basics. o IT: Computer fundamentals, Python basics. o Analytics: Excel, Power BI basics. o HR: Recruitment and payroll fundamentals. o Marketing: SEO and digital marketing. o Finance: Banking, budgeting and financial literacy. 4. Stage 4 – Communication and employability o Spoken English o Interview preparation o Presentation skills o Group discussions o Resume preparation o Logical reasoning and aptitude 5. Stage 5 – Practical exposure o Prepare a sample business report. o Create an Excel accounting statement. o Practise a presentation. o Understand how companies operate. o Participate in business competitions or supervised projects. 4. Preparation after 2nd PUC – Three-year career development plan A student should follow a planned learning path throughout graduation. Year Academic qualification Additional preparation 1st year B.Com / BBA / BCA Computer skills, Excel, English, basic certification 2nd year Continue degree Professional software, domain certification, projects 3rd year Complete degree Internship, interview training, resume, placement preparation For example: B.Com student – Accounting career • First year: Excel, Tally Prime. • Second year: GST, TDS, bookkeeping and payroll. • Third year: Practical accounting, internship and interview preparation. BBA student – Business Analyst career • First year: Excel and communication. • Second year: Power BI, SQL and business reporting. • Third year: Business projects, internship and interview preparation. BBA student – HR career • First year: MS Office and communication. • Second year: Recruitment, HR operations, payroll. • Third year: HRMS, labour law fundamentals and internship. 5.
